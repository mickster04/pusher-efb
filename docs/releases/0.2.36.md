# Push Planner 0.2.36

Pre-release patch on [0.2.35](0.2.35.md). One new setting, found from sim feedback that the tug was too slow in turns. See also the [first-release notes draft](../release-notes.md) and the [release checklist](../release-checklist.md).

## Changes

- **Speed in turns** (Settings > Pushing). How fast the tug goes round a curve:
  - **Usual** (the default) keeps the behaviour of earlier versions: the tug slows more the tighter the curve is, and brakes ahead of each curve. Tight turns on small aircraft (the ATR and Caravan turn at about 20 m radius) ran at the tug's minimum of 1.6 ft/s (about 1 kt).
  - **60, 70, 80, 90 or 100%** sets the curve speed as that percentage of the straight-line speed, whatever the radius. **100%** does not slow the tug for curves at all.
  - It follows "Maximum cruise speed", so raise that too for a faster push overall. It applies to a push already under way.
  - The tug still slows when it points well away from the line, when it is more than 5 m off the path, and to a crawl at the end of each leg, and it never goes below 1.6 ft/s.
- **Packaging.** A Google Drive duplicate of the localization folder (`PackageSources/localization (2)`) that had been committed by mistake is removed from the repository.
- The setting's label and note are translated into German, French, Spanish (Spain) and Spanish (Mexico).

## Known issues

As in [0.2.35](0.2.35.md). In addition, faster turns have not been checked for tracking: if the tug drifts off the line in a turn, step the setting down (80% is a sensible first try).

## Verified

Unit tests pass (154), including the curve speed at Usual, 80% and 100%. Not yet run in the sim.
